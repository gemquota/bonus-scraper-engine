Colour Lab

Live preview of the viewer's colour scheme. Categories (level 1) nest subtypes (level 2), and every subtype carries up to three shades (level 3: soft / base / deep). Column groups nest all 88 individual columns (level 2), each with its own hue slider — columns you leave alone inherit their group hue, and ones you edit are tagged custom. Use the palette generator to distribute any number of hues across the legend with a growing spacing curve, or drag the accordion sliders directly. Copy the generated values into src/colors.js (TYPE_RULES / COLUMN_GROUPS / COL_HUES) to make them permanent.
